Facilities Telemetry & Derived Physical Proxy Metrics

Headcount & Occupancy: Primary ground-truth physical metric measured across all facilities.
Energy, CO2, and Wi-Fi Probes: Secondary physical proxies modeled from occupant metabolic emissions (100W sensible thermal heat, 0.005 L/s CO2 per person in ASHRAE 62.1 spaces) and 1.24x device-to-person probe ratios.

Dataset Provenance & Temporal Holdout Split Methodology

Dataset Size: 15,120 records (90 calendar days × 24 hourly intervals × 7 campus facility zones).
Chronological Split: Days 1–72 (80%, N = 12,096) strictly for training; Days 73–90 (20%, N = 3,024) held out as strictly future unseen test data.
Zero Cross-Period Data Leakage: Random cross-validation is barred for time-series telemetry because random shuffling leaks same-day future hours into training. The chronological holdout guarantees evaluation on unseen future temporal horizons.
